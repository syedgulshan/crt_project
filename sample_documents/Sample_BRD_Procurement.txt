================================================================================
BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)
PROJECT: Enterprise Governance & Customer Operations
VERSION: 1.8 | COMPLIANCE LEVEL: REGULATED
================================================================================

Section 1.0 Executive Vision & Governance
This document codifies corporate standards, compliance controls, and customer-facing
operational policies mandated across enterprise business units.

Section 2.0 Identity & Access Policy
BRD-014: Password recovery must only be performed through administrator verification and manual approval ticket.
BRD-045: Users can change their profile information including contact preferences, bio, and associated details.

Section 4.0 User Session & UX Standards
BRD-021: Users must remain logged in for at least 30 minutes without activity before any forced session termination.

Section 5.0 Fulfillment Logistics & Cancellation Policy
BRD-033: Orders may be cancelled by customers until dispatch notification is emitted from warehouse facilities.

Section 6.0 Financial Invoicing & Reporting
BRD-060: Customer invoice reports must be generated and downloadable in standard PDF and CSV formats within 5 seconds.

Section 7.0 Security Mandates & Infrastructure
BRD-052: All corporate storage volumes and relational database records must be encrypted at rest utilizing AES-256.

Section 8.0 Legal Compliance & Audit Trails
BRD-080: Audit logs must capture every authorization attempt and be immutable for a minimum retention of 7 years.

Section 9.0 SLA & Performance Guidelines
BRD-071: System response latency shall be adequate and comfortable for enterprise branch office operators.
